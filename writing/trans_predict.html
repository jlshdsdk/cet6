第1篇 文房四宝

Four Treasures of Study

文房四宝作为中国传统文化中独具特色的书写工具组合，承载着数千年华夏文明的深厚底蕴。这 一称谓特指笔、墨、纸、砚四种基本文具，它们在中国文人的精神世界里占据着不可替代的重要地位。 毛笔以其独特的柔软笔锋著称，能够表现出丰富的线条变化；墨依其质地可分为松烟墨与油烟墨两大 类，在不同历史时期各有发展；宣纸因其卓越的吸墨性与耐久性成为书画创作的首选材料；砚台作为 研磨墨块的器具，其材质选择与工艺制作同样极为讲究。文房四宝不仅是实用工具，更是中华文化审 美意识与哲学思想的集中体现，在世界文化宝库中具有独一无二的价值。

参考译文

The Four Treasures of Study, as a uniquely distinctive combination of writing tools in traditional Chinese culture, carry the profound heritage of Chinese civilization spanning thousands of years. This term specifically refers to four basic stationery items: the brush, ink, paper, and inkstone, which occupy an irreplaceable position in the spiritual world of Chinese scholars. The writing brush is renowned for its unique soft tip, capable of expressing rich line variations; ink can be divided into two major categories according to its texture: pine soot ink and oil soot ink, each developing in different historical periods; Xuan paper has become the preferred material for calligraphy and painting due to its excellent ink absorption and durability; the inkstone, as a tool for grinding ink sticks, demands extremely sophisticated material selection and craftsmanship. The Four Treasures of Study are not only practical tools but also a concentrated embodiment of Chinese cultural aesthetic consciousness and philosophical thought, holding unique value in the world's cultural treasure house.

第2篇 茶马古道

Tea-Horse Road

茶马古道作为中国西南地区古代最重要的商贸通道之一，其线路网络错综复杂，主要由滇藏线与川藏线两 条主干线路构成，全长累计可达数千公里之遥。这条古道因以茶叶与马匹为主要交易商品而得名，其开通运营 对于加强内地汉族地区与边疆少数民族聚居区之间的经济联系发挥了举足轻重的作用。从历史文化价值角度审 视，茶马古道不仅是一条物质交换的商旅通道，更是一条促进民族文化交融互鉴的纽带，沿途各民族在长期交 往过程中相互影响、共同发展，形成了多元共生的文化景观。茶马古道沿线的文化遗产保护与可持续旅游开发 问题已成为当前学术界与政府部门共同关注的热点问题。

参考译文

The Ancient Tea-Horse Road, one of the most important ancient trade routes in southwestern China, features a complex network primarily composed of two main corridors —the Yunnan-Tibet and Sichuan-Tibet routes—stretching thousands of kilometers in total. Named for its primary traded goods—tea and horses—this route played a crucial role in strengthening economic ties between inland Han Chinese regions and frontier areas inhabited by ethnic minorities. From a historical and cultural perspective, the Tea-Horse Road was not merely a commercial passage for material exchange but also a vital link fostering cultural interaction and mutual learning among diverse ethnic groups. Through long-term interactions, these communities influenced one another and developed together, creating a richly diverse and coexisting cultural landscape along the route. Today, the protection of cultural heritage and sustainable tourism development along the Tea-Horse Road have become key issues attracting attention from both academic circles and government agencies..

第3篇 川剧变脸

Sichuan Opera Face-changing

川剧变脸作为中国传统戏曲艺术中最具神秘色彩与视觉冲击力的表演绝技，堪称中国戏剧艺术宝库中的一 颗璀璨明珠。变脸技艺相传源于古代祭祀仪式中巫师佩戴面具驱邪祈福的神秘习俗，历经数百年民间艺术实践 的积淀与提炼，逐渐演变为舞台表演艺术的重要组成部分。从艺术表现层面分析，变脸技法可分为抹脸、吹脸、 扯脸等多种类型，其中扯脸因其技术难度最高、视觉效果最为震撼而最具代表性。变脸演员须经过长期艰苦的 专业训练，方能熟练掌握快速变换面部彩绘或面具的精湛技巧。值得关注的是，变脸技艺历来被视为行业机密， 传承体系相对封闭，这一特点更为其增添了浓郁的神秘色彩。如今，川剧变脸已成为对外传播中华文化的重要 艺术符号。

参考译文

As the most mysterious and visually impactful performance skill in traditional Chinese opera art, Sichuan Opera face-changing can be described as a brilliant pearl in the treasure house of Chinese dramatic art. The face-changing skill is said to originate from the mysterious customs of witches wearing masks to ward off evil and pray for blessings in ancient religious ceremonies. After several centuries of accumulation and refinement in folk art practice, it has gradually evolved into an important component of stage performing arts. From the perspective of artistic expression, face-changing techniques can be divided into several types such as smearing, blowing, and pulling, among which pulling face is the most representative due to its highest technical difficulty and most shocking visual effect. Face-changing performers must undergo long-term arduous professional training to master the superb skills of quickly changing facial paintings or masks. It is worth noting that face-changing skills have always been regarded as industry secrets, and the inheritance system is relatively closed, which adds a strong mysterious color to this art form. Today, Sichuan Opera face-changing has become an important artistic symbol for spreading Chinese culture internationally.

第4篇 无人机应用

Drone Applications

无人机技术作为新一代信息技术与航空产业深度融合的产物，近年来在技术创新突破与商业应用拓展方面 均取得了令人瞩目的进展。从技术分类维度，无人机按动力系统可划分为多旋翼、固定翼与垂直起降等多种构 型，不同机型适用于差异化的应用场景需求。在农业植保领域，植保无人机凭借其作业效率高、喷雾覆盖均匀、 施药成本低等显著优势，已逐步替代传统人工植保方式成为主流技术路线。在物流配送领域，无人机配送在解 决偏远地区"最后一公里"配送难题方面展现出独特价值，但在续航里程、载荷重量与飞行安全管控等方面仍面 临技术瓶颈制约。

参考译文

As a product of the deep integration between next-generation information technology and the aviation industry, drone technology has achieved remarkable progress in recent years, both in technological innovation and commercial application expansion. From a technical classification perspective, drones can be categorized by power system into various configurations such as multirotor, fixed-wing, and vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL), each suited to different application scenarios. In agricultural protection, crop protection drones have gradually replaced traditional manual methods as the mainstream solution due to their high operational efficiency, uniform spray coverage, and low pesticide application costs. In logistics and delivery, drone-based delivery demonstrates unique value in addressing the "last-mile" challenge in remote areas; however, it still faces technical limitations regarding flight endurance, payload capacity, and flight safety management.

第5篇 数字化转型

Digital Transformation

数字化转型作为数字经济时代企业战略变革的核心命题，已从早期的技术应用层面 上升至组织管理乃至商业模式创新的系统化工程高度。从本质内涵而言，数字化转型 并非简单地以数字技术替代传统人工操作，而是要实现业务流程的数字化重构与数据 要素的价值释放。此外，中小企业因面临资金短缺与技术储备不足等现实约束，其数 字化转型进程相对滞后，如何帮助中小企业跨越数字化转型鸿沟已成为亟待解决的重 要议题。

参考译文

Digital transformation, as a core issue in corporate strategic evolution during the digital economy era, has evolved from an initial focus on technological applications to a systematic endeavor encompassing organizational management and even innovation in business models. Fundamentally, digital transformation is not merely about replacing traditional manual operations with digital technologies, but rather about digitally reconstructing business processes and unlocking the value of data as a key resource. Moreover, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), constrained by limited funding and insufficient technological capabilities, have relatively lagged behind in their digital transformation efforts. How to help SMEs overcome the digital divide has thus become a pressing and critical issue requiring urgent attention.
