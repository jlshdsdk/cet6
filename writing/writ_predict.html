第1篇 年轻人过度依赖短视频

Directions: Nowadays, an increasing number of young people spend plenty of time on short-video platforms, which has triggered widespread concern. Write an essay to analyze the causes of this phenomenon and put forward your suggestions.
范文

In the digital era, short videos have penetrated every corner of people’s daily life. It is noticeable that numerous young people become addicted to short videos, wasting massive spare time on browsing various fragmented contents. This undesirable phenomenon deserves our high attention. Several fundamental factors contribute to this trend. To begin with, short videos feature rich forms, diverse topics and entertaining contents, which can help young people relieve study and work pressure in a short time. Furthermore, compared with lengthy articles, fragmented video content is easier to understand and accept, catering to the fast-paced lifestyle of contemporary youngsters. Last but not least, many young people lack self-discipline, so they are unable to control their browsing time effectively. From my perspective, it is urgent to take effective measures to address this issue. For one thing, young people should establish a correct entertainment concept and arrange their time reasonably, striking a balance between relaxation and self- improvement. For another, relevant platforms ought to optimize content push mechanism and remind users to use the product rationally. Only in this way can young people benefit from short videos rather than being trapped by them.

要点笔记

1. This undesirable phenomenon deserves our high attention.（这一不良现象值得我 们高度重视） 2. Strike a balance between relaxation and self-improvement.（在放松与自我提升之 间寻求平衡）

第2篇 人工智能对职场的影响

Directions: With the rapid development of artificial intelligence, AI has been widely applied in various industries and even replaced some human jobs. Please write an essay and state your views on the impact of AI on the job market.
范文

Artificial intelligence, one of the most revolutionary technologies in the 21st century, is profoundly changing the global job market. People hold divergent opinions on whether AI will bring more opportunities or threats to employees. As far as I am concerned, AI is a double-edged sword for the workplace. On the one hand, AI does pose certain challenges to traditional jobs. A host of repetitive and mechanical positions, such as data entry and basic customer service, are gradually being taken over by intelligent machines, leading to the unemployment of part of workers. On the other hand, the popularity of AI also creates new employment opportunities. The emerging AI-related posts, including algorithm engineers and data analysts, provide new career choices for high-quality talents. To adapt to the changing job market, individuals need to make positive changes. We should abandon the fear of technological change, keep learning professional knowledge and master comprehensive skills that AI cannot replicate, like critical thinking, innovative ability and emotional communication. In brief, embracing AI and upgrading ourselves is the best way to cope with future career changes.

要点笔记

1. AI is a double-edged sword for the workplace.（人工智能在职场中是一把双刃剑） 2. Embrace technological changes and realize self-upgrading.（拥抱技术变革，实现 自我升级）

第3篇 弘扬中华传统文化

Directions: In recent years, traditional Chinese culture has regained popularity among young people, such as Hanfu, traditional festivals and ancient poetry. Write an essay to discuss the significance of inheriting traditional Chinese culture.
范文

Recently, the revival of traditional Chinese culture has become a prevalent social trend. More and more young people start to wear Hanfu, celebrate traditional festivals and learn traditional folk arts. This positive trend carries far-reaching significance for our country and younger generations. Firstly, traditional culture is the spiritual treasure of the Chinese nation and the core of national soft power. Inheriting excellent traditional culture helps enhance national cultural confidence and national cohesion. Secondly, traditional culture exerts a subtle influence on teenagers’ growth. Excellent cultural essence can shape people’s values and cultivate their sense of national identity. Thirdly, traditional culture can also promote cultural exchange worldwide, helping the world know more about China. In my opinion, cultural inheritance requires joint efforts of the whole society. Schools should add traditional culture courses to enrich students’ cultural knowledge. Meanwhile, young people are supposed to take the initiative to learn traditional culture and innovate its presentation forms. Only by passing down traditional culture from generation to generation can we maintain our unique cultural characteristics.

要点笔记

1. Traditional culture is the spiritual treasure of the Chinese nation.（传统文化是中华 民族的精神瑰宝） 2. Exert a subtle influence on one’s personal growth.（对个人成长产生潜移默化的影 响）

第4篇 践行绿色低碳生活

Directions: Environmental pollution and resource waste remain severe problems globally. Low-carbon lifestyle has been advocated by all sectors of society. Please write an essay about how college students can practice green and low-carbon life.
范文

With the deterioration of the global ecological environment, environmental protection has become a global consensus. Currently, the concept of low-carbon life has been widely promoted, and it is every citizen’s obligation to live a green lifestyle, especially for college students. There are various practical measures for college students to go green. To begin with, in daily life, students should save resources, including turning off unused electrical equipment, saving water and avoiding food waste. In addition, it is advisable for students to choose low-carbon travel modes such as walking, cycling and public transport instead of private cars. Furthermore, students can actively participate in environmental protection volunteer activities to spread green concepts to people around them. In conclusion, ecological protection is a long-term project that cannot be accomplished overnight. Small daily behaviors accumulated by each individual will make a huge difference to environmental improvement. College students should take the lead in practicing low-carbon life and contribute to building an eco-friendly society.

要点笔记

1. Environmental protection has become a global consensus.（环境保护已成为全球 共识） 2. A long-term project that cannot be accomplished overnight.（一项绝非一蹴而就的 长期工程）

第5篇 培养终身学习能力

Directions: In the era of knowledge explosion, knowledge updates at an unprecedented speed. Therefore, lifelong learning is essential for modern people. Write an essay to explain the importance of lifelong learning and how to develop this ability.
范文

We are living in an era of knowledge explosion, where new knowledge and skills emerge every day. Under such a background, lifelong learning is no longer an optional choice but a necessary quality for modern people to survive and develop. The importance of lifelong learning can be reflected in two aspects. On the one hand, continuous learning enables individuals to keep pace with the times, update their knowledge reserve and enhance comprehensive competitiveness in the fierce job market. On the other hand, lifelong learning can enrich people’s spiritual world, broaden their horizons and help them realize personal value in multiple fields. As for college students, we can cultivate lifelong learning ability from two aspects. For one thing, we need to abandon the outdated mindset that schooling equals the end of learning, and form the awareness of active learning. For another, we should formulate reasonable learning plans, combine professional study with interest learning, and absorb new knowledge persistently. In short, lifelong learning is the key to long- term personal development.

要点笔记

1. Keep pace with the times and update knowledge reserve.（紧跟时代步伐，更新知 识储备） 2. Lifelong learning is the key to long-term personal development.（终身学习是个人 长远发展的关键）
