How to Eliminate Appearance Anxiety

People are now increasingly aware of the danger of "appearance anxiety" or being obsessed with one's looks. Appearance anxiety can have a detrimental effect on one's mental and physical wellbeing. It can lead to depression, anxiety, eating disorders and other health issues. It can also cause some people to spend excessive amounts of money on cosmetic procedures, products and fashion items, leading to financial difficulties.

To eliminate appearance anxiety, it's essential to acknowledge that everyone is unique and there is no one-size-fits-all standard of beauty. It is crucial to promote self-love and self-acceptance, and encourage people to embrace their individuality and appreciate themselves for who they are. Additionally, self-care practices such as healthy eating, regular exercise, enough sleep and relaxation can improve overall wellbeing and boost self-confidence. Besides, if appearance anxiety is significantly affecting your life, seek the help of a mental health professional.

In summary, eliminating appearance anxiety requires a combination of positive self-talk, self-care and professional help if necessary. By taking these steps, you can overcome appearance anxiety and lead a happier and healthier life.

听力原文 · Recording One

It has long been scientifically established that weather changes can affect people's moods. (16) Now, a new study has provided evidence that temperature can influence people's personalities. This study of over 1.6 million people revealed that 22 degrees Celsius is the perfect air temperature to live in. A city with an average annual temperature closer to 22 tends to have a population who are more agreeable, conscientious, emotionally stable and outgoing. It is the least taxing temperature for the body to regulate its own temperature. The study was observational and didn't show cause and effect, but the scientists behind it theorized that better weather leads people to leave their home more often. This, in turn, leads to more social interaction, which encourages them to develop a friendlier and socially more acceptable personality.

(17) Warmer climates also make people feel more positive in general. They tend to be more agreeable and conscientious. The findings might help explain why colder and warmer countries tend to produce people with different personalities. Roughly speaking, about 40% of a person's personality is determined by their genes, the other 60% by their environment. It was already well known that personality traits vary across geographic regions. Scientists also knew that these geographic personality traits are associated with a broad range of consequential outcomes. These outcomes include economic activity, for example, entrepreneurial start-up rates, and also crime rates, health behaviors and health outcomes.

And it is well established that personality traits differ between countries. The research team speculated the two might be linked. To test this, they gave online personality tests to 5,587 Chinese students and 1.66 million Americans. They then compared the results with the average annual temperature where they grew up. The tests measured personality along five well-studied characteristics. The five were agreeability, conscientiousness, emotional stability, outgoingness, and openness to new experiences. In both groups, the researchers found the closer a town's average annual temperature was to 22 degrees, the more its population exhibited those personality characteristics. However, (18) the findings were much stronger for the Chinese group than the Americans studied, which suggests that though temperature plays a role, it does not play a dominant role. The effects are fairly weak. It's unlikely to lead to many arguments over the temperature studying of the office air conditioner.

此外,性格特质因国家而异,这也是确凿的。研究团队推测这两者可能存在联系。为验证这一点,他们对5587名中国学生和166万名美国人进行了在线性格测试。然后他们将测试结果与测试对象成长地的年平均气温进行对比。测试沿五项研究充分的特质来测量性格。这五项是随和性、尽责性、情绪稳定性、外向性和对新事物的开放性。在这两组人中,研究人员都发现,一个城镇的年平均气温越接近22度,其居民就越多地表现出那些性格特征。然而,(18) 研究结果在中国组中比在所研究的美国人中明显得多,这表明温度虽然起作用,但并不起主导作用,其影响相当微弱。它不太可能引发许多关于办公室空调温度研究的争论。
