Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence "As is known to all, gaining a sound knowledge of the basics is of vital importance for students to master an academic subject." You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words (not including the sentence given).
写作范文（点击展开）

As is known to all, gaining a sound knowledge of the basics is of vital importance for students to master an academic subject. I fully agree with the above opinion. I have two arguments to support it.

First of all, learning the basics helps students be prepared to master an academic subject. As everyone knows, learning is a step-by-step process. Students should start with the basic knowledge of an academic subject before they are able to reach a higher level. Moreover, the knowledge of the basics contains the universal law of a subject, which can be applied to different branches of the academic subject. Thus gaining a sound knowledge of the basics is necessary for mastering the knowledge of various branches of the academic subject.

In conclusion, gaining a sound knowledge of the basics is extremely important for students to master an academic subject. It can not only help students be prepared for the future study but also help them master the knowledge of different branches of the academic subject.

范文译文

人们普遍认为，获得扎实的基础知识对学生掌握一门学科至关重要。我完全同意上述观点。我有两个理由来支持这一观点。

首先，学习基础知识有助于学生为掌握一门学科做好准备。众所周知，学习是一个循序渐进的过程。学生应该从一门学科的基础知识开始，然后才能达到更高的水平。此外，基础知识包含了一门学科的一般规律，可以适用于该学科的不同分科。因此，获得扎实的基础知识对于掌握该学科各个分科的知识是必要的。

总之，获得扎实的基础知识对于学生掌握一门学科是极其重要的。它不仅可以帮助学生为以后的学习做好准备，还可以帮助他们掌握该学科不同分科的知识。

Her books have frequently been described as comedies of manners. 听力原文 · Recording One

What makes humans different from other species? Some philosophers argue it's morals or ethics, while some scientists assert it's our greater cognitive development. But I argue that the main difference is our desire to combat routine. This makes being creative a biological mandate, as what we seek in art and technology is surprise, not simply a fulfillment of expectations.

As a result, a wild imagination has characterized the history of our species. We build intricate habitats, devise complex recipes for our food, wear clothes that reflect constant changes in fashion, communicate with elaborate signs, symbols, and sounds, and travel between habitats on wings and wheels of our own design. To satisfy our appetite for novelty, innovation is key. But who innovates?

Now, many people, both laymen and experts, believe that only geniuses innovate. But I believe that innovation is not something that only a few people do. The innovative drive lives in every human brain, and the resulting war against the repetitive is what powers the massive changes that distinguish one generation from the next. The drive to create the new is a trait of being human. We build cultures by the hundreds and tell new stories by the millions. We create and surround ourselves with things that have never existed before, while animals do not. But where do our new ideas come from?

According to many, new ideas come from seemingly nowhere to great minds. From this perspective, new ideas are almost like magic. They come in a flash of inspiration to a select few. However, the reality is that, across the spectrum of human activities, prior work propels the creative process. We may think of innovation as being the result of inspiration or genius, but it's really the result of developing the ideas of others further. This happens in technology, where one invention enables or inspires further inventions. And it happens in the arts, as writers, composers, and painters use the work of previous artists in their own work. The human brain works from precedent. We take the ideas we've inherited and put them together into some new shape.

What is a true creator? Is a creator a genius who makes something out of nothing? No. Creators are simply humans who use what they inherit, who absorb the past and manipulate it to create possible futures. Thus, humans are creators as a rule rather than as an exception.

原文中文译文

是什么让人类不同于其他物种？一些哲学家认为是道德或伦理，而一些科学家则断言是人类更发达的认知。但我认为，主要的区别是我们对抗例行公事的欲望。这使得创造成为一种生物学上的必然，因为我们在艺术和技术中寻求的是惊喜，而不只是预期的实现。

因此，丰富的想象力贯穿了我们这个物种的历史。我们建造精巧的居所，为食物设计复杂的配方，穿着反映时尚不断变化的衣服，用复杂的记号、符号和声音交流，乘坐自己设计的翅膀和轮子在居所之间穿行。为了满足我们对新奇事物的渴望，创新是关键。但谁是创新者？

如今，许多外行和专家都认为只有天才才能创新。但我认为，创新并不是只有少数人才能做的事情。创新的动力存在于每个人类大脑中，由此产生的对抗重复的战争，正是推动一代与一代之间巨大变化的力量。创造新事物的驱动力是人类的一种特质。我们创造出数以百计的文化，讲述数以百万计的新故事。我们创造并置身于从未存在过的事物之中，而动物不会这样做。但我们的新想法从何而来？

在许多人看来，新想法仿佛凭空出现在伟大人物的头脑中。从这个角度看，新想法几乎像魔法，在灵光一闪之间降临到少数被选中的人身上。然而，事实是，在人类活动的各个领域，都是先前的工作推动着创造过程。我们可能认为创新是灵感或天才的结果，但它其实是进一步发展他人想法的结果。这发生在技术领域，一项发明促成或启发更多的发明；也发生在艺术领域，作家、作曲家和画家在自己的作品中借用前人的成果。人脑依托先例运作。我们把继承来的想法组合在一起，形成新的形态。

什么是真正的创造者？创造者是无所不能、无中生有的天才吗？不。创造者不过是利用自己所继承之物的人，是吸收过去并加以改造以创造可能的未来的人。因此，人类是常态意义上的创造者，而不是例外。
